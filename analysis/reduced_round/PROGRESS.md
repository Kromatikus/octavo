Goal: find the most rounds of live Octavo (NR = 16) that differential, linear, integral/square and simple algebraic tests can distinguish or break, and justify 16 rounds with a safety margin.

Target: /workspace/sneak_n2/sneak_n2.py, read-only, SHA-256 19181bc1… (asserted by harness.py before every import). Do not modify live code or frozen snapshots. No pushes, no posting. Don't draw the sneak-staff picture.

Model: white-box rounds under random lab seeds+nonces (several keys per test), via harness.enc_r(block, sch, rounds). Note each message gets a fresh Schedule (seed+nonce), so attacks are per-schedule chosen-plaintext — state this caveat.

- Stage 1 (stage1_diffusion.py, stage1b_subround.py; outputs stage1*_output.txt / .json): full byte dependency after 1 round (every input byte -> every output byte; mean active 127.5/128 = random). Bit SAC 1024x1024 indistinguishable from random after 1 round (512 samples/cell), and already at the half-round point (after the first cross_staff_mix, before S2) at 256 samples/cell. Single-shell: a difference confined to one shell reaches the other shell ~13% after the shell bridge, ~48% after odd_restack, 99.6% (random) after the first cross_staff_mix -> no single-shell confinement survives half a round.
- Stage 2 (stage2_differential.py, stage2b_peeled.py): keyed S-boxes DDT max 4 (DP 2^-6), LAT max 16 (|corr| 2^-3) on 12 sampled boxes. Raw outputs: no single-byte/bit/inner-shell/2-byte difference distinguishes even 0.5 round at 2^16 pairs x 3 schedules (byte chi-square + bit z, threshold z>5). With the free peel (final cross_staff_mix is unkeyed and XOR-linear, so it can be inverted on output differences), 0.5 round is trivially distinguished (72 always-zero difference bytes; positive control works). 1, 1.5, 2 rounds peeled: nothing (also 2^20 pairs at r=1). Max differential: 0.5 round. Rigorous trail bound: >=1 active keyed S-box in each of 4 S-box layers per round (S, mid B0/odd Latin, S2, encore Latin; odd_restack pairs cover every beat) -> trail EDP <= 2^-24 per round; empirically ~127 of 128 bytes are active entering S2 after a 1-byte start.
- Stage 3 (stage3_linear.py, stage3b_control.py; helper stage2b_peeled_lib.py): 2044 input masks (1024 single bits + 4 bytes x 255 byte masks) vs 1024 single output bits, 2^18 plaintexts x 2 schedules per level. 0.5 raw/peeled, 1, 1.5, 2 peeled: max z 4.9-5.3, consistent with the null (~5.4 expected max over 2M statistics); nothing over threshold 6. Positive control: right after the first S-layer the test finds |corr| 0.138 (z 35); after the butterfly single-bit output masks already lose it. Max linear: < 0.5 round with this mask family (only through the first S-box layer). LAT bound: trail |corr| <= 2^-12 per round.
- Stage 4 (stage4_integral.py v2; v1 partial run kept as stage4_run1_partial_output.txt): 8 trials per set, per-trial balanced-byte counts (random ~0.5/128). 1 active byte: all 128 bytes balanced through the bar layer; after the first cross_staff_mix, peeling it back gives 72-76 balanced bytes (min 72) -> 0.5-round integral. After S2 (0.6 round) and at 1, 1.5, 2 rounds: random (mean 0.1-0.9). 2 active bytes (same face, or one per shell): 0.5 round peeled min 88-96 balanced; S2 kills it. 2^24 set (3 bytes, one face), one trial: r=1 peeled 2/128 balanced, after bow2 0/128 -> random. Max integral: 0.5 round (+ free peel). No shell-specific balance (inner/outer counts symmetric).
- Stage 5 (stage5_algebraic.py v2, stage5b_fullround_check.py, stage5_lib.py; v1 output kept as stage5_run1_*): cube zero-sums, d = 4..20, packed (bits filling bytes of one face) and spread (1 bit per byte), 3 trials each. Spread cubes: full zero-sum on all 1024 bits from d=8 (run 1: from d=6) at the half-round point (peeled cross) -> degree ~4-5 there; packed cubes: 78-98% zero-sum bits at half round (many output bits independent of the cube). After S2 (0.6 round), after bow2, at 1 and 2 rounds peeled: zero-sum fraction 0.49-0.52 = random at every d up to 20. Max algebraic (empirical): 0.5 round. Theory: degree <= ~5*7 = 35 right after S2 would allow a ~2^36 zero-sum at ~0.6 round (not tested); B0/odd2 keyed S-boxes push past that inside round 1.
